Every lane measured live and authenticated, every recommendation pushed through an adversarial second pass before it reached this page. Two of my own draft proposals died in that pass. What survived is below, ranked, with the numbers that back it.

01 · Verdict

The engine outclasses its surface

Under the paint this is a serious tool: 228 KB of JavaScript, first paint in 644–844 ms, zero console errors on every lane at every width, honest empty states, and a draft-review window whose QA provenance would hold up next to anything commercial. The engineering is done.

The surface undersells it in one specific, measurable way: the app is set small and laid out empty at the same time. Body copy runs 13–14 px as if pixels were scarce, while the Content lane paints 703 characters on a 1440×900 screen and a 2560 monitor leaves roughly 65% of the plate black. You have asked for readability three times (08-04, 08-07, and the Flow screenshot this week). The prior passes moved type one step; this plan finishes the job.

One rule shaped every recommendation here: your Flow screenshot is a readability exhibit, and it gets a readability answer. The skin you picked in the 08-02 ballot stays untouched unless you vote otherwise in a side-by-side at the end of this page.

04 · System defects, ranked

What the census caught

Ranked by how much daily reading cost they carry. A-severity items change how the app feels every hour; C-severity are craft debts.

F01 · TYPE REGISTER DRIFT

30 live type combinations on the lane surfaces

The token file declares a 7-tier scale. The DOM renders 30 distinct family·weight·size·line-height combinations: the 14px tier alone runs line-heights 14, 20, 21 and 22; the 13px tier runs 16, 18, 18.9, 19.5 and 20. Five line-heights on one size is why surfaces feel subtly unrelated even though every screen was individually designed. The code check traced each value to its rule, including the 18.9 (a stray 13px × 1.45 at faithful.css:3849), and counted 18 distinct sizes surviving the cascade across the sheets; the lanes render 6 of them.

14px → lh 14 · 20 · 21 · 22 13px → lh 16 · 18 · 18.9 · 19.5 · 20 lane sizes: 11 · 13 · 14 · 16 · 20 · 30 sheet-wide: 18 sizes survive the cascade
F02 · THE MECHANICAL CAUSE

The stylesheet wins by repetition, so every new style must out-shout it

faithful.css flattens everything (.wb.wb, .wb.wb * forces one size), then re-assigns tiers at triple specificity. The repo's own comment records the consequence: any new selector must be written .wb.wb.wb or it silently loses its type. The code check counted 1,605 triple-class selectors and 29 !important declarations riding this war; every drift item in F01 is its shrapnel.

The verification pass also sized the cure honestly. @scope cannot fix this (scope proximity sits below specificity; the flattener would win anyway). @layer can, and it needs a three-layer split with screenshot baselines before and after: a real refactor, worth doing while already in the file, never as a quick win. The quick wins live in the platform table.

F03 · TODAY RENDERS LOGS, PLUS DUPLICATES

The first screen of the day is machine output

Confirmed in the live DOM: the same bennett-ca warning ships twice byte-identical, six warnings share one identical body without grouping, and the CRITICAL card concatenates a WARN block inside its own string. Numbers that deserve tiles (1 sent vs ~50 expected) are buried mid-sentence in 90ch+ run-ons.

F04 · NO KEYBOARD LAYER

Every triage action is a mouse trip

Global bindings today: ⌘D (voice) and Escape. j/k lives only inside the draft and magnet windows (the magnet window also keeps s and o). No palette, no list navigation, no shortcut sheet. One history note the plan respects: the draft window's a/r/e/s quick-action keys were removed on 08-09 as unneeded, so re-adding action keys ships only with your word; the palette and navigation keys carry no such history.

F05 · DISCARD IS FOREVER, AND ONE PATH IS UNGUARDED

Terminal writes with no recovery, one latent send bug

Discard writes a terminal block with no restore anywhere in the UI; the stale-bar bulk fires N of them behind one confirm, and composing a reply silently discards the pending AI draft with no confirm at all. The verification pass also found a latent bug: discardDraft checks only sent_at IS NULL. Landing it on an already-approved row writes the block columns, while the dispatcher (which never reads them) still sends the message, and the row vanishes from the UI. One-line guard plus a restore action closes the whole class.

F06 · CONTRAST FLOOR MISSES

Two token pairs fail their roles

Measured against WCAG: text4 #7E7E7E on cards reads 4.06:1, under the 4.5 body floor (passes for large text only, and it is used at 12–13px). In the light theme the lime accent renders at 1.20:1 on white: the lane chips are functionally invisible. Light stays opt-in per the decision log, and this bug still deserves the fix.

#7E7E7E on #1F1F1F → 4.06:1 (body floor 4.5) #B8FF66 on #FFFFFF → 1.20:1 (light theme chips)
F07 · SPACING AND RADII DRIFT

35 padding pairs · 10 rendered radii · 25 radius literals in source against 5 tokens

The census counts 35 distinct top/left padding pairs (five of them near-identical: 10/14, 10/16, 10/12, 11/16, 9/16) and rendered radii at 8, 10, 12, 13, 14, 16, 20, 28, 40, 999px; the 10, 13, 14 and 16 values bypass the token set. The source check found it runs deeper: 25 distinct border-radius literals in the sheets against 5 declared tokens, including a 99px and a 999px pill living side by side. Same story as F01: token discipline exists on paper and the cascade lets literals through.

F08 · MOBILE REGRESSIONS

Word-breaks in KPI labels, 15×20px dismiss targets

At 390px the Sends funnel prints ACCEP TED and CONVO S; Today's dismiss buttons are 15×20px against the 44px target rule this app already adopted on 08-03; mobile row medians run 233px (Ops) and 317px (Sends), roughly 2.5 rows per screen.

F09 · WIDE CANVAS UNUSED

860px cap on lists, a 640px ribbon in the takeover

At 2560: DMs leaves ~65% of the plate empty (the list children cap at 860px, styles.css:35-40); in the draft window the middle column grows to ~1,968px while its content rides a 640px centered ribbon, leaving ~660px of dead gutter on each side. The measure cap belongs on prose; panes should split the surplus, and in the takeover the surplus should go to the inspector first.

F11 · THE ERRORS TAB HAS NO ERRORS COLUMN

46 failed drafts, 44 with no visible reason

Two rows carry a real verdict; the rest render a dash. Finding out why a draft failed means opening it, once per row. The verdict data exists (it drives the chips that do appear), so this is a rendering gap, and the same label map that fixes F12 supplies it.

F12 · RAW DATABASE VALUES ON SCREEN

Eight surfaces print the column, not the word

Dm_sent · dm_sent · thread_already_answered · LEAD_MAGNET · youtube_watch · QA_BLOCKED · LINT_FAIL · gold_icp_v2_seatless. Each one is a place the operator has to translate machine vocabulary in their head. One shared label map, one afternoon.

F13 · DM HISTORY BALLOONS THE PAGE

One click takes the busiest lane from 2,499 to 59,452 characters

All 213 conversations inline, unvirtualized, controls 12 → 225, and the expanded state persists across reloads. Pagination or a capped window with a "show more" is the fix; this is the one lane where list volume is real.

F14 · THE MAGNET QUEUE RAIL INVERTS ITS HIERARCHY

Titles cut at 14 characters while metadata wraps to four lines

In a 232px rail, "Interactive Assessment · 26d ago" outranks the title it belongs to, and row heights swing 60→110px as a result. Two lines of title, one quiet line of meta, fixed row height.

F15 · SMALL HONESTY LEAKS

Emoji severity, hash-first receipts, stray build label

🔴 as a severity glyph on alerts; Ops booked rows lead with #C0BJ72F58BY before the person; Settings floats "Build 6db1ae7" mid-canvas; errored rows still offer Approve at full weight. Ten-minute fixes, real register cost.
